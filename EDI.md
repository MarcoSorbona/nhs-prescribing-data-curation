# Equality, Diversity and Inclusion

## My Commitment

Diversity means bringing different perspectives to the table. I am committed to creating a workplace where everyone can contribute fully, regardless of background, disability, neurodiversity, or circumstance.

## In My Work

### Accessible Outputs

-   All visualisations use colour‑blind friendly palettes (e.g., viridis)
-   Interactive plots include hover tooltips and clear labelling
-   Clear, plain‑language interpretation for non‑technical and public audiences
-   Quarto reports render to accessible HTML

### Health Inequalities Focus

-   Regional prescribing variation analysis (e.g., East Midlands vs national benchmarks)
-   Deprivation as a confounder (demonstrated in UKHSA environmental PM2.5 project)
-   Focus on equitable healthcare access and outcomes
-   EPD data supports monitoring of prescribing equity across demographic and geographic groups

### Workplace Practice

-   I ensure meetings are inclusive (rotating chairs, asynchronous options, clear agendas)
-   I support reasonable adjustments for colleagues (e.g., flexible hours, screen readers)
-   I challenge bias and microaggressions when I see them
-   I believe diversity strengthens science and improves health outcomes

## Continuous Learning

-   I stay informed about EDI best practices (e.g., NHS EDI improvement framework)
-   I seek feedback on my inclusive practice
-   I recognise that this is an ongoing commitment, not a checkbox
